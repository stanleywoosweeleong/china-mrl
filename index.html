02 · RESIDUE REQUIREMENTS

Does the shipment meet the applicable limits?

Match the commodity, the residue definition, the sampling portion and the current limit. Have the laboratory assess analytes, metabolite sums, reporting limits and measurement uncertainty.

How to read a result

Direct entry: the table names the commodity. Group entry: the table names a food group that includes the selected commodity, after exclusions. The details show the original Chinese wording and Annex A reference.

Provisional (*): a provisional limit, not an LOQ marker. EMRL: an extraneous maximum residue limit, generally addressing environmental residues. Neither permits use of a prohibited pesticide.

Missing limits and non-detects

No numeric match does not mean zero, unrestricted use, or a default 0.01 mg/kg. Check the entire standard and Annex B, and resolve applicability with your importer or competent authority. A non-detect must be interpreted with its laboratory reporting limit.

Fresh does not mean processed

Frozen fruit, purées, juices and dried produce need the appropriate food classification and rules. Do not automatically apply a fresh-fruit lookup to another product form. Use the entire-standard view to inspect other source entries.

Documented access is conditional evidence, not shipment clearance. “Not verified” means this app has no reviewed matching record; it does not mean prohibited.

Before arranging a shipment

  1. Confirm current country–commodity access and any suspension with GACC and the exporting country’s plant-health authority.
  2. Verify the orchard, packing house and any treatment facility are on the applicable current registers.
  3. Check the full protocol, permits, phytosanitary certificate, treatment, packaging and entry-port requirements.
  4. Assess residue limits separately for the exact product and residue definition.
