# Market-access catalogue review — 10 November 2026

The app's country catalogue is an index of reviewed evidence, not a comprehensive current permission registry. No missing combination is classified as prohibited. All positive records remain conditional, and operational clearance is explicitly unverified.

## Malaysian expansion

- GACC 2023 No. 34, issued 11 April 2023, supports fresh Malaysian jackfruit. Reviewed the MOFCOM republication, including registration, bagging, certification and entry requirements: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2023/art_1e5c78e3e65d45f2812d3f9a1f49b21c.html
- FAMA's *Kit eksport produk agromakanan 2025*, Table 1, PDF pages 8–11, lists the eight fresh fruits represented in the app. Added five guide-backed entries; retained the more specific protocol evidence already recorded for coconut and durian. The guide edition is displayed as 2025, not misrepresented as a new 2026 approval. Full source URL is in market-access.json.
- Bernama's 2 July 2026 report of DOA's statement on overland durian exports through transit countries is linked as supplemental route information, not a country-wide durian suspension: https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2575755

The DOA 2026 horticultural export PDF and GACC master-list endpoint could not be retrieved in this session. Secondary global lists were not imported as verified official records. No conclusion about Malaysian vegetable permissions, or completeness for any country, was drawn. Future maintenance must check current protocols, suspensions, approved facilities and routes rather than simply refreshing the document-review date.

## Expansion, 10 November 2026 (second pass)

- GACC's own permitted-fruit list (customs.gov.cn) could not be read: the site refused automated requests (HTTP 412/503).
- 290 fresh-fruit records across 60 origins come from Produce Report's edited English translation of that list
  (https://www.producereport.com/market-access-list, "Last updated Oct. 6, 2026"). They carry `evidenceType: "list-translation"`
  and are labelled as a translation everywhere they appear. `announced` is the translation's update date.
- 11 records come from GACC announcements republished by MOFCOM or the Fujian commerce department (`evidenceType: "protocol"`).
  Their conditions are paraphrased summaries, not verbatim text.
- Mapping choices: "Mandarin (Citrus reticulata)" is recorded for both 柑 and 橘; generic "Citrus spp." (EG, US, UY) for 橙/柑/橘/柠檬
  (UY without 柠檬), not 柚; "Melon" only for 甜瓜. Region, cultivar and trial-import limits are in each record's scope.
- Excluded: entries the list marks suspended (Taiwan pineapple, atemoya, wax apple; Turkey cherry), North Korea (processed only),
  an ambiguous "DOMINICA" avocado entry, Uganda chilli (dried only), Laos sweet potato (processing use, page not read).
- Not in the crop catalogue, so not recorded: grapefruit, limes, clementine/tangelo, kiwi berry, tamarind, longkong, sapodilla,
  salak, Indian jujube, dates, betel nut.
- Fresh vegetables: only Vietnam chilli, Vietnam sweet potato and Tanzania chilli were found. Suspensions were not researched
  beyond what the translated list flags.
