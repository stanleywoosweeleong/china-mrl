## Malaysian expansion

- GACC 2023 No. 34, issued 11 April 2023, supports fresh Malaysian jackfruit. Reviewed the MOFCOM republication, including registration, bagging, certification and entry requirements: https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2023/art_1e5c78e3e65d45f2812d3f9a1f49b21c.html
- FAMA's *Kit eksport produk agromakanan 2025*, Table 1, PDF pages 8–11, lists the eight fresh fruits represented in the app. Added five guide-backed entries; retained the more specific protocol evidence already recorded for coconut and durian. The guide edition is displayed as 2025, not misrepresented as a new 2026 approval. Full source URL is in market-access.json.
- Bernama's 2 July 2026 report of DOA's statement on overland durian exports through transit countries is linked as supplemental route information, not a country-wide durian suspension: https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2575755

The DOA 2026 horticultural export PDF and GACC master-list endpoint could not be retrieved in this session. Secondary global lists were not imported as verified official records. No conclusion about Malaysian vegetable permissions, or completeness for any country, was drawn.
